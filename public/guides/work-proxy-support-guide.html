1 Who We Are & What We Sell

Work Proxy helps people access and succeed on global freelance / AI-training platforms — Outlier AI, OneForma, and Handshake — through guided onboarding, training, and managed accounts.

Managed pricing works differently
The prices above are for standalone / self-managed service. When Work Proxy manages the account and does the tasks, the model switches to a revenue share (see below) rather than these one-off fees. Always confirm which model the client wants first.

Two pricing models — managed vs. self-managed

Pricing depends on whether Work Proxy manages the account or not. Always establish this first, because it changes both the price and how earnings are split.

✓ Work Proxy managed

  • We set up and run the account — our team does the tasks.
  • Earnings are shared 50/50 between the client and Work Proxy.
  • No upfront package fee — we earn from the share, so we're invested in the results.
  • Best for clients who can't work the account themselves (e.g. students, busy clients).

○ Self-managed (client works it)

  • We register / train / onboard, then the client works the account themselves.
  • The client keeps 100% of their earnings — no revenue split.
  • Pricing is the one-off package fee only (see table above).
  • Best for clients abroad who want to do the work themselves.
The revenue share applies to managed accounts only
If a client asks "what's the margin / sharing ratio?" — that only applies when Work Proxy manages the account and does the tasks. Standard share is 50/50. A self-managed client keeps 100% of their earnings and just pays the package fee.
Negotiation floor — start at 50/50, never go below 40/60
Always quote 50/50 first. If a client pushes hard for a bigger cut, you may go down to a maximum of 40/60 in the client's favour (40% to Work Proxy, 60% to the client) — that is the absolute floor. Do not offer 40/60 upfront; only use it to close a serious client who is pressing for more.
Your selling point for the share
Justify the value confidently: "Our team works the account 12 hours a day, consistently averaging $1,300–$1,500 per week. You earn that hands-free while we do all the work — that's why the split is worth it."
Always confirm current pricing
Prices, packages, and which model applies can change. First ask "Will Work Proxy be managing the account, or will you work it yourself?" — then quote the matching price. If unsure a rate is still active, say "Let me confirm the current rate for you" and check with the team before quoting.

2 The 5 Golden Rules

  1. Eligibility before anything else. The person whose account will be used must be located outside Nigeria (e.g. UK, US, Canada, EU). If they're in Nigeria with no one abroad, we cannot help — say so kindly and close.
  2. Verify there's a real project. Before running an onboarding/assessment package, ask for a dashboard screenshot showing a live project. No project = we can't run the package.
  3. Never collect money in DMs without confirming the order. Only share official payment details after the customer's details and eligibility are confirmed.
  4. One customer, one thread. Never forward or reveal another customer's name, email, or chat.
  5. If you don't know, don't guess. Say you'll confirm and escalate. A wrong promise costs us more than a slow answer.

3 Tone & Voice

✓ Do sound like

  • Friendly, calm, confident
  • Short sentences, one idea per message
  • Helpful even when the answer is "no"
  • Professional English (light, not stiff)

✗ Avoid

  • Rude or blunt replies ("I can't help you")
  • Long walls of text
  • Over-promising income or approval
  • Slang that confuses non-Nigerian clients
Greeting standard
Open every new chat with a warm line before business: "Hello! Thanks for reaching out to Work Proxy 👋 I'd be happy to help."

4 Qualifying Every Lead

Run this quick checklist in your head on every booking. Ask the questions naturally, one at a time.

  1. Location: "Where is the account holder based?" → must be outside Nigeria.
  2. Access: "Do you (or someone you trust) have access to that device/location?"
  3. Project: For onboarding/assessment work — "Please send a screenshot of your dashboard so I can confirm there's a project to onboard."
  4. Managed or self-managed? "Will Work Proxy be managing the account, or will you work it yourself?" — this sets the price and whether the 50/50 share applies.
  5. Service fit: Match what they need (registration, training, assessment, management) to the right package.
  6. Confirm details: Name, email, platform, package — read it back before payment.
Disqualify cleanly
If the account holder is inside Nigeria and they have no one abroad, do not take payment. Decline politely (see reply 5E) — never just say "I can't help you."

5 Ready-to-Send Replies

Copy, tweak the name, and send. These cover the most common situations.

5A New booking request comes in
Hello! 👋 Thanks for reaching out to Work Proxy. Before we proceed, one quick question: where is the account holder currently based? Our platforms require the account to be operated from outside Nigeria, so I just need to confirm eligibility first 🙂
Why: Eligibility first — it saves everyone time and protects the account.
5B Customer is eligible (outside Nigeria) — move forward
Perfect, that works ✅ Here's what I have for your booking: • Service: [SERVICE] • Platform: [PLATFORM] • Name: [NAME] • Email: [EMAIL] Is that all correct? Once you confirm, I'll share the next step and payment details.
Why: Reading details back prevents mistakes and feels professional.
5C Onboarding / assessment package — ask for proof of project
Great! For this package, please send a screenshot of your dashboard so I can confirm you have a live project to onboard. We need an active project to run this package — otherwise it can't be completed. Once I see it, we're good to go 👍
Why: No project = no package. Always verify before charging.
5D "Should I just open the account, or include specific info?"
Good question! Don't open it blindly — the setup details matter for approval and getting matched to a project. We guide the process step by step to make sure the account is set up correctly and actually lands a project. Would you like Work Proxy to manage the account for you, or just guide you through it?
Why: Turns a vague question into a clear choice between two services.
5E Account holder is inside Nigeria / no one abroad — decline kindly
Thanks for your honesty 🙏 Right now our platforms require the account to be operated from outside Nigeria, so we're unable to set this up if there's no one abroad to hold the account. The moment you have someone based outside Nigeria, reach back out and we'll get you started straight away 👍
Why: Says no without burning the lead — they may qualify later.
5F "Where do I pay?" — share details only after confirming
Awesome 🙌 Here are the payment details for [SERVICE] — [AMOUNT]: [OFFICIAL PAYMENT DETAILS] Once payment is done, send the receipt here and we'll begin your onboarding right away.
Why: Confirm order + eligibility before sharing payment info. Use only official, approved payment details.
5G "What's the margin / sharing ratio?"
Good question 🙂 That applies to managed accounts — where our team runs the account and does the tasks for you. The split is 50/50. And it's genuinely worth it: our team works the account about 12 hours a day, consistently averaging $1,300–$1,500 per week. You earn that hands-free while we do all the work. If you'd rather work the account yourself, there's no split — you keep 100% of your earnings and just pay the one-off package fee. Which would you prefer?
Why: Answers the ratio, leads with the value, and steers them to pick a model.
If they push for more: hold at 50/50, then only as a last resort to close, you may go to a max of 40/60 in their favour. Never open with 40/60.
5H Following up on a quiet lead
Hi [NAME] 👋 Just checking in on your Work Proxy booking — are you still interested in getting started? Happy to answer any questions before you decide 🙂
Why: Warm, low-pressure nudge. One follow-up, then leave it.

6 Handling Objections

"It's too expensive."

Reframe to value, never argue: "I understand. This isn't just account access — it's training, setup, and ongoing support so you actually earn on the platform. Most clients make the fee back quickly once they're active."

"Can you guarantee I'll make money / get approved?"

Be honest: "We can't guarantee income or approval — no one honestly can. What we guarantee is correct setup, training, and support to give you the best possible chance."

"Why must I be outside Nigeria?"

"It's a platform-level requirement — verification, payouts, and compliance are tied to eligible countries. It's not our rule, it's theirs, and we follow it to keep accounts safe."

"Is this legit / a scam?"

Stay calm and confident: "Totally fair to ask 🙂 We're Work Proxy (workproxy.fun). I'll walk you through every step, and you only pay once you're clear on what you're getting."

7 Payment & Next Steps

  1. Confirm eligibility ✅ and the exact package + price.
  2. Read the customer's details back and get a "yes".
  3. Share only official, approved payment details.
  4. Ask for the receipt/screenshot of payment.
  5. Confirm receipt, then hand off to onboarding and tell the customer what happens next and roughly when.
Never
Never give out your personal account, accept payment to an unapproved number, or ask for platform passwords over chat in an unsafe way. If a customer pushes for shortcuts, escalate.

8 Hard Do's & Don'ts

✓ Always

  • Confirm location is outside Nigeria first
  • Verify a live project before assessment packages
  • Be polite, even when declining
  • Read details back before payment
  • Escalate anything you're unsure about

✗ Never

  • Promise income or guaranteed approval
  • Share another customer's info
  • Take payment to unapproved accounts
  • Reply rudely ("I can't help you")
  • Invent prices or policies on the spot

9 When to Escalate

Loop in the team lead immediately if:

  • A customer claims a payment issue, refund, or chargeback.
  • Someone requests something outside our standard packages.
  • An account gets flagged, suspended, or banned.
  • A customer is angry, threatening, or alleges fraud.
  • You're ever unsure whether to say yes — pause and ask.
Holding line while you check
"Great question — let me confirm that for you and get right back to you 🙂" Then escalate. Customers respect "let me check" far more than a confident wrong answer.
