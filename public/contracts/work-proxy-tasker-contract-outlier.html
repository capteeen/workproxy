2 Purpose & Scope

The Tasker agrees to perform task work on Outlier AI accounts assigned and managed by Work Proxy. The Tasker will complete tasks diligently, follow all platform and Work Proxy guidelines, and work to maintain the account in good standing (high ratings, no violations, no project removals).

Outlier-specific
This contract covers Outlier AI only. Separate terms apply for any other platform (OneForma, Handshake, etc.).

3 Reporting & Hierarchy

  1. Work Proxy has appointed Ibrahim Ismail Maikaita as Team Lead to oversee all tasker work. The Tasker reports directly to the Team Lead.
  2. All questions, updates, issues, and reports must go to the Team Lead — not directly to the CEO, unless instructed otherwise.
  3. The Tasker agrees to follow the Team Lead's instructions and to treat the Team Lead with the same respect owed to the Company.
  4. Professional, respectful communication is required at all times.

5 Rules & Code of Conduct

The following rules are mandatory. Breaching any of them may result in strict action, including suspension, withheld pay, or removal.

Rule 1 · No shared logins
No Tasker is permitted to log into another Tasker's PC or account. Every Tasker works only their own assigned device/account so that, if anything goes wrong, the source can always be identified.
Rule 2 · Follow the hierarchy
Report to your Team Lead (Ibrahim Ismail Maikaita), not the CEO. Follow the Team Lead's instructions and communicate respectfully at all times.
Rule 3 · Stay active · Minimum 10 hours daily
Taskers must work a minimum of 10 hours every day and remain consistently active. Falling below the daily minimum or inactivity without prior notice may result in removal from the team.
Rule 4 · Protect the account
Follow all Outlier and Work Proxy guidelines to keep accounts in good standing. Do not engage in any activity that risks account suspension, low ratings, or project removal. We cannot afford to lose accounts.
Rule 5 · Confidentiality
Account credentials, internal processes, client information, and Work Proxy methods are confidential and must never be shared with anyone outside Work Proxy.
Rule 6 · Honesty & integrity
No fraud, no falsifying work, no manipulation of earnings or ratings. All work must be genuine and to the required quality standard.

6 Term, Breach & Termination

  • This Agreement begins on the date of signature and continues until terminated by either Party.
  • Work Proxy may suspend or remove a Tasker immediately for any breach of the rules in Section 5.
  • Strict action will be taken against any Tasker who violates this contract, including withholding of pay and permanent removal.
  • Earnings genuinely accrued up to the termination date will be paid in the next regular weekly cycle, subject to Section 4.
  • On termination, the Tasker must immediately stop all access to and use of Work Proxy accounts and return/erase any credentials.

7 Independent Contractor Status

The Tasker is engaged as an independent contractor, not an employee. The Tasker is responsible for their own taxes and any local obligations. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership or employment relationship.
