## 1. What Work Proxy Is

Work Proxy is a **managed account-sharing marketplace** that bridges global, geo-restricted
work platforms (like Outlier AI and OneForma) with skilled workers in regions that can't
access them directly (primarily Nigeria).

**The core idea:**
- **Account Owners** in the UK/US have idle accounts on high-paying platforms.
- **Workers** (e.g. in Nigeria) are skilled but geo-blocked from those platforms.
- **Work Proxy** sits in the middle: it vets both parties, generates contracts,
  holds funds in escrow, stores credentials securely, tracks performance, and
  mediates disputes.

**One-line pitch:** *"Bridge the Gap Between Global Platforms & Local Talent."*

### The three parties
| Party | What they do | What they earn |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| **Account Owner** (UK/US) | Lists a platform account, sets split, proves earnings | 30–40% passively |
| **Work Proxy** (platform) | Vets, contracts, escrow, performance, disputes | 50% always |
| **Worker** (e.g. Nigeria) | Applies, signs agreement, accesses account, does the work | 10–30% by tier |

## 3. How It Works (End-to-End Flow)

1. **Account Owner Lists** — A UK/US holder submits a platform account, sets their
   preferred revenue split, and verifies authenticity with earnings proof.
2. **Work Proxy Matches** — We vet both parties, generate the Account Management
   Agreement, set up escrow, track performance, and mediate disputes.
3. **Worker Manages** — A verified worker applies, signs the agreement, gets credential
   access **only after both parties sign AND escrow is funded**, does the work, and earns weekly.

## 14. Legal & Risk (FAQ Answers)

**Is this legal?**
Work Proxy operates as a professional account management service. We provide legal
disclaimers, and contracts clearly define liability. The platform is not responsible for
platform-ToS decisions — users accept that risk through the agreement.

**How do workers receive credentials?**
Only after both parties digitally sign the Account Management Agreement **and** the owner
funds escrow. Credentials are AES-256 encrypted and accessed via the secure vault.

**What if an account gets banned?**
The contract has a liability clause. If the ban is due to **worker negligence**, the worker
bears liability. If it's a **platform-wide action**, neither party is liable. Admin mediates the review.

**How fast do workers get paid?**
Weekly or bi-weekly based on verified earnings, via Payoneer, Grey, Geegpay, or USDT.

## 18. Support Bot Guidance ("Claw")

- **Tone:** Friendly, clear, professional. Reassure on security (escrow, AES-256, contracts).
- **Never promise** guaranteed income or guaranteed platform approval — the product itself avoids this.
- **Route money/registration/referrals through official channels only** — the bot must never
  ask users to send fees to individuals; all payments go through Work Proxy.
- **For account-specific issues** (payout status, matches, disputes, recovery), collect the
  user's email/details and escalate to a human admin via WhatsApp/email.
- **Geo-rule reminder:** Account holders must be based outside Nigeria; workers are typically
  in Nigeria. Don't conflate the two roles.
- **Pricing is in Naira (₦) for onboarding/academy and USD ($) for RDP** — quote the right currency.
- When unsure or asked something not covered here, **direct the user to WhatsApp support**
  rather than guessing.
